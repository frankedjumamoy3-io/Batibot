# Curriculum World Review Board Audit

Date: 2026-06-21

Role: Curriculum World Auditor

Scope: Preschool through Grade 3 world structure, topic sequencing, progression, Active Learning Framework implementation, cognitive alignment, activity suitability, MATATAG foundational literacy alignment, and MATATAG foundational numeracy alignment.

## Executive Summary

BatiBot now has a coherent grade-to-world-to-topic curriculum path. Preschool and Kindergarten are the strongest because their worlds are concrete, visual, audio-supported, and developmentally close to recognition, tapping, matching, counting, and pattern completion. Grade 1 is aligned at the topic level but needs stronger specialized activities beyond matching and choice. Grade 2 and Grade 3 contain the right curriculum targets, and specialized renderers now exist for major upper-grade domains, but several still behave like structured choice activities rather than true manipulatives or reasoning workspaces.

Overall curriculum-world alignment: 7.3/10.

## Final Curriculum Progression Answer

If a learner completed Preschool to Kindergarten to Grade 1 to Grade 2 to Grade 3, they would experience a generally clear cognitive progression:

- Preschool: concrete visual/audio recognition, tap, match, count, compare, and pattern.
- Kindergarten: symbols, letter sounds, oral vocabulary, teen numbers, shapes, and patterns.
- Grade 1: words, simple sentences, early number concepts, operations, shapes, and time.
- Grade 2: vocabulary relationships, comprehension, place value, measurement, multiplication, and word problems.
- Grade 3: context clues, story elements, fractions, division, money, and multi-step operations.

However, some worlds still behave like lower-grade activities with higher-grade labels because their current implementation depends too much on matching, choosing, or compact template questions instead of grade-level manipulatives and reasoning workspaces.
