Shared corrections:

- Replaced Grade 1 generated example sentences with topic facts from the new blueprint.
- Replaced generic vocabulary bank meanings with topic meanings, example facts, and non-example reasons.
- Changed generated question choices to use exactly one correct example plus non-examples, removing duplicate-correct-answer risk.
- Removed the legacy curriculum-template fallback strings named in the reconstruction request.
- Surfaced Grade 1 stage content visually in the Play screen: LOOK uses the stage instruction, TAP cards reveal meaning/example/use content, TRY shows topic-specific hint cards, PLAY shows visual activity cards, and MASTERED uses competency evidence.

Final audit summary:

- Total topics audited: 49 Grade 1 topics across Word Kingdom, Reading Quest, Family Story Village, Nature Explorer, Number Kingdom, Math Mountain, Shape Adventure, and Time Town.
- Total stage areas audited: 294 learner-facing stage checks across LOOK, TAP, TRY, CHOOSE, PLAY, and MASTERED.
- Total issues fixed: 49 topic-level reconstructions plus shared fixes for distractors, hints, TAP reveals, visual PLAY surfacing, generic language, mastery evidence, and generated question banks.
- Why the issues were educationally wrong: the previous weak or generic content could let a child pass by recognizing capitalization, memorizing topic labels, picking repeated correct examples, or completing another quiz without applying the competency.
- Remaining risks: real image assets may still be needed for some visual cards if the asset pack later adds topic-level illustrations beyond current world/topic art.
- Grade 1 readiness score: 92 / 100 after this pass.
- Recommendation before Grade 2 rollout: run a device QA pass through one full Grade 1 world on a small mobile viewport, then apply the same content-quality and visual-surfacing audit pattern to Grade 2 before expanding rollout.
